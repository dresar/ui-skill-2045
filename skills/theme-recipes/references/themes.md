## Depth rules

### Soft Blue Neumorphism

Two opposing shadows: one dark blue, one white. No borders. Pressed state inverts the shadows.

Dua bayangan berlawanan: biru gelap dan putih. Tanpa border. State pressed membalik bayangan.

### Glassmorphism

Translucent surface + 1px light border + backdrop blur. No drop shadows except for true overlays.

Permukaan tembus pandang + border terang 1px + backdrop blur. Tanpa drop shadow kecuali overlay sungguhan.

### Aurora Gradient

No shadows. Depth comes from overlapping color fields and scale.

Tanpa bayangan. Kedalaman datang dari bidang warna yang tumpang tindih dan skala.

### Minimal Monochrome

Tone steps and 1px rules. No shadows.

Langkah tone dan garis 1px. Tanpa bayangan.

### Brutalist Modern

Hard borders and offset solid blocks. Shadows are solid, never blurred.

Border keras dan blok solid bergeser. Bayangan solid, tidak pernah blur.

### Cyber Neon

Glow only on the accent. Surfaces use tone steps and 1px borders.

Glow hanya pada aksen. Permukaan memakai langkah tone dan border 1px.

### Dark Neumorphism

Paired shadows: near-black below, lighter gray above. No borders except on focus.

Bayangan berpasangan: hitam di bawah, abu terang di atas. Tanpa border kecuali saat focus.

### Bento Grid

Panel tone and gap. One border weight. No shadows.

Tone panel dan jarak. Satu ketebalan border. Tanpa bayangan.

### Editorial Serif

Rules and type scale. No shadows.

Garis dan skala tipe. Tanpa bayangan.

### Claymorphism

Soft outer shadow plus a subtle inner highlight. One light direction.

Shadow luar lembut plus highlight dalam halus. Satu arah cahaya.

### Blueprint Grid

1px rules and a background grid. No shadows. Elevation is a lighter panel.

Garis 1px dan grid latar. Tanpa bayangan. Elevasi adalah panel lebih terang.
